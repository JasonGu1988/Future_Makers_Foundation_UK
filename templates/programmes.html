Royal Albert Hall

Music for young people

Future Makers Foundation supports Engagement at the Royal Albert Hall which changes lives and provides inspiration through music for all, regardless of age or background. In particular Future Makers Foundation supports the Hall’s work with Young People aged 16-25 which aims to develop the next generation of artists and music industry leaders by offering one-of-a-kind experiences to those who would not otherwise have access to such resources and opportunities.

Jigsaw4u

Supporting vulnerable children

Future Makers Foundation has forged a link with the London Charity Jigsaw4u and commenced a program to assist them in helping vulnerable children suffering from domestic abuse, child sexual exploitation, family bereavement and other social and psychological problems.
